OUR STORY

A Little Corner for Good Food & Good Coffee

Your Neighbourhood Café in Silvassa

Kaaafi Korner is a cozy neighbourhood café serving specialty coffee, South Indian favourites, and a selection of classic café dishes. From a good cup of coffee and a dosa to sandwiches, breakfasts and easy comfort food, there’s something for everyone. Come by for a quick coffee, a meal with friends, or simply to spend a little time at the Korner.
